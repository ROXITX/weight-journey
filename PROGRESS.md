## Data model (Firestore)
`users/{uid}` → `{ profile, goals, habits[], notifications, settings, onboarded }`
`users/{uid}/dailyLogs/{YYYY-MM-DD}` → habits{}, steps, workouts[], food{overate, overeatReasons, sugaryDrinks, dietDrinks, meals[]}, bowel, sleep{bed,wake,hours}, journal{}, mood, energy, hunger
`friendCodes/{CODE}` → {uid, name} · `publicStats/{uid}` → shared SUMMARY + friendCode (no journal/food/raw logs) · `publicStats/{uid}/followers/{fid}` → fid may read it
`users/{uid}/weightLogs/{date}` → {date, weight, note} · `waterLogs/{date}` → {date, totalMl, entries[]} · `achievements/{id}`
